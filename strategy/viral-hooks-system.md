# Viral Hooks System

Hooks work when they create fast relevance.

## Hook Jobs

A good hook should do at least one of these:

- Name a painful problem.
- Challenge a common belief.
- Promise a useful outcome.
- Reveal a hidden mistake.
- Make the viewer curious.
- Show a specific transformation.

## Hook Patterns

### Problem

- "You are not stuck because you lack ideas. You are stuck because..."
- "If your content feels random, check this first."
- "This is why your posts are not landing."

### Mistake

- "The biggest mistake people make with carousels is..."
- "Stop starting your content with..."
- "This looks helpful, but it actually weakens your post."

### Reframe

- "Consistency is not posting more. It is..."
- "Better content does not start with better wording. It starts with..."
- "Your niche is not the topic. It is the problem you solve."

### Outcome

- "Use this to turn one idea into five posts."
- "A simple way to make your caption easier to read."
- "Try this when your content idea feels too vague."

## Hook Quality Check

- Is it specific?
- Does it create tension?
- Can the audience recognize themselves in it?
- Does the content actually deliver on it?
