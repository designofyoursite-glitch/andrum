# High-Performing Emotional Structures For Reels

Use these as repeatable Reel formats for Andrum.

## 1. Tiny Trigger To Full Spiral

Best for: no reply, cold text, vague tone, seeing them online.

Structure:

1. Name the tiny trigger.
2. Name the body reaction.
3. Name the story the mind creates.
4. Offer one grounding reframe.
5. End with a small next step.

Example:

> They do not reply for three hours. Your chest tightens. Your mind says, "They are losing interest." Before you send the message, pause and ask: did something happen, or did my body just remember an old fear?

## 2. The Thing You Think You Want Vs The Thing You Actually Want

Best for: reassurance, closure, going back, checking phone.

Structure:

1. "You think you want..."
2. "But what your body is really asking for is..."
3. Translate the need into plain language.
4. Give a non-shaming action.

Example:

> You think you want the text. But what your body wants is the feeling that the connection did not disappear.

## 3. Relationship Contradiction

Best for: situationships and mixed signals.

Structure:

1. Present the contradiction.
2. Make the emotional cost visible.
3. Name why it is confusing.
4. Give a clarity line.

Example:

> They say they do not want anything serious, but they act like your partner when you are together. Of course your body is confused. Warmth without clarity keeps you waiting for the part that never arrives.

## 4. Shame To Recognition

Best for: "too much", self-blame, reassurance shame.

Structure:

1. Name the shame thought.
2. Interrupt it.
3. Offer a kinder, sharper truth.
4. Give language for the next time.

Example:

> The thought says, "I am too much." But sometimes what is actually happening is: you are asking for steadiness from someone who keeps making you guess.

## 5. Body First, Meaning Second

Best for: breathwork posts.

Structure:

1. Start with a sensation.
2. Connect it to a real moment.
3. Explain in simple human language.
4. Offer one breath/body action.

Example:

> If your stomach drops when they go quiet, start with the body. Longer exhale. Feet on the floor. Do not ask your mind for clarity while your body is still in alarm.

## 6. The Familiar Spiral

Best for: going back to exes, chasing unavailable people.

Structure:

1. Name the repeated pattern.
2. Show the emotional logic.
3. Reveal the hidden payoff.
4. Offer a new interruption.

Example:

> You keep going back because the spiral is familiar. Not because it is good for you. Familiar can feel safer than empty, even when it hurts.

## 7. The Line You Needed To Hear

Best for: saveable, shareable Reels.

Structure:

1. Start with "If..."
2. Name the painful situation.
3. Deliver one emotionally precise truth.
4. End without overexplaining.

Example:

> If you have to become less honest, less needy, less visible, and less yourself to keep the connection, the connection is already costing too much.

## 8. Calm Is Not Boring

Best for: hopeful endings and healing arcs.

Structure:

1. Contrast chaos with calm.
2. Name why chaos used to feel magnetic.
3. Reframe calm as safety.
4. Close with a soft future-facing line.

Example:

> At first, calm love can feel like nothing is happening. That does not mean it is empty. It may mean your body is not fighting for proof anymore.

## 9. One Message You Do Not Send

Best for: inner monologue and restraint.

Structure:

1. Show the message they want to send.
2. Show the fear underneath it.
3. Rewrite it into a steadier truth.
4. Suggest waiting until the body settles.

Example:

> The message says, "Are we okay?" The fear says, "Please prove I am not being left." Wait until the panic drops. Then decide what actually needs to be said.

## 10. Before / After Inner Shift

Best for: transformation without sounding fake.

Structure:

1. "Before, I..."
2. "Now, I..."
3. Keep both emotionally honest.
4. Avoid perfection.

Example:

> Before, inconsistency made me try harder. Now, it makes me pause and ask whether this is the kind of connection my body can actually rest inside.

## Reel Performance Notes

Strong Reels in this world usually include:

- A recognizable first line.
- A phone/texting/relationship micro-scene.
- A body sensation.
- A private thought.
- A contradiction.
- One reframe.
- One small action, not a whole healing plan.
