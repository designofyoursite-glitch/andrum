# Tone of Voice

Andrum should sound clear, calm, intelligent, and useful.

## Voice Traits

- Grounded, not dramatic.
- Direct, not harsh.
- Thoughtful, not academic.
- Warm, not overly emotional.
- Practical, not generic.

## Writing Rules

- Use simple language.
- Say one clear thing at a time.
- Prefer examples over abstractions.
- Cut filler phrases.
- Make every post feel like it was written for a real person.

## Good Andrum Lines Feel Like

- "Here is the part most people miss."
- "This looks small, but it changes the whole result."
- "If you are stuck, start with this."
- "The goal is not more content. The goal is better signals."

## Avoid

- Hype language.
- Empty inspiration.
- Corporate jargon.
- Overexplaining.
- Trying to sound profound.
