# Content Pillars

Use these as the main lanes for Andrum content.

## 1. Clarity

Helping people sharpen ideas, messaging, and positioning.

Examples:

- How to turn a vague idea into a clear post.
- Signs your message is too broad.
- Simple questions that improve positioning.

## 2. Content Systems

Helping people create repeatable workflows instead of starting from zero.

Examples:

- Weekly content planning.
- Turning one idea into multiple formats.
- Building a swipe file.

## 3. Creative Confidence

Helping people publish with less overthinking and more trust in their process.

Examples:

- Why your first draft feels bad.
- How to post before everything feels perfect.
- What consistency actually means.

## 4. Audience Insight

Helping people understand what their audience needs, notices, and responds to.

Examples:

- How to find audience pain points.
- Comments as content research.
- Why saves and shares matter.

## 5. Better Execution

Helping people make stronger Reels, carousels, captions, and hooks.

Examples:

- Hook rewrites.
- Carousel structure.
- Caption cleanup.
