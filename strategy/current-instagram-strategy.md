# Current Instagram Strategy

Project: Andrum

Stage: audience discovery and early pattern testing.

## Project Context

Andrum is a new English-speaking Instagram account in the anxious attachment, emotional regulation, and feminine inner world niche.

Original content territory:

- Nervous system regulation.
- Breathwork.
- Emotional processing.
- SOS practices for women with anxious attachment.
- Body-based healing.

Early testing showed that educational breathwork and nervous system content performs poorly as front-facing Instagram content.

The strongest reactions come from:

- Painfully self-aware feminine monologues.
- Messy contradictory thoughts.
- Emotionally uncomfortable self-recognition.
- Anxious attachment spirals.
- Hyper-specific emotional behaviors.

## Current Strategic Hypothesis

Andrum should first build audience resonance and algorithmic clustering through highly relatable emotional content before introducing deeper breathwork or nervous system education.

The audience needs to feel:

> "This is literally me."

Before they are ready for:

> "Here is how breathwork helps."

## Primary Content Priority

The primary goal is not education.

The primary goal is emotional recognition and relatability.

If forced to choose between psychological precision and emotional recognition, always prioritize emotional recognition.

This means:

- Start from the exact behavior.
- Write for fast recognition.
- Keep the thought internet-native.
- Avoid explaining the mechanism too early.
- Make the viewer feel seen before making them understand.

## Emotional Realism

The content should feel emotionally exposed, not emotionally polished.

Prefer:

- Awkward honesty.
- Contradiction.
- Irrational emotional logic.
- Self-awareness.
- Messy inner experiences.

Avoid:

- Resolved wisdom.
- Emotionally perfect conclusions.
- Clean healing narratives.

## Body-First Filter

Whenever possible, translate emotional states into body experiences:

- Chest tightness.
- Urge to check the phone.
- Stomach drop.
- Adrenaline spike.
- Numbness.
- Relief waves.
- Dissociation.
- Shaking.
- Hyperfocus.

Do not stay only in abstract thoughts.

## Anti-AI Writing Rule

Avoid:

- Symmetrical sentence structure.
- Generic emotional summaries.
- Repetitive validation phrases.
- Obvious AI phrasing.
- Content that sounds optimized but emotionally empty.

The writing should feel specific, human, slightly imperfect, and emotionally lived-in.

## Current Content Mix

### 70% Simple Relatable Emotional Hooks

Use for reach, retention, saves, shares, and audience clustering.

Focus:

- Simple emotional situations.
- Specific anxious attachment behaviors.
- Short hooks.
- Low cognitive load.
- Instantly understandable emotional recognition.
- Shareable and saveable phrasing.

Examples:

- "what's it called when one dry text ruins your entire personality for the day"
- "you keep rereading the same conversation like there's a hidden sentence where he finally chooses you"
- "you keep checking your phone like maybe this time he'll suddenly become emotionally available"

### 30% Deeper Messy Contradictory Monologues

Use for identity, depth, emotional loyalty, and differentiation.

Focus:

- Emotionally uncomfortable nuance.
- Self-aware anxious attachment spirals.
- Contradiction.
- Shame recognition.
- Internal conflict.
- Thoughts that feel irrational but true inside the body.

## Important Strategic Principle

Do not fully pivot into generic motivational content.

Andrum's unique strength is not:

> "choose yourself queen"

It is:

> messy, emotionally self-aware contradiction made simple enough to instantly understand.

The goal is to simplify delivery without flattening the emotional identity.

## Early Performance Signals

### Best Early Performing Reel

Text:

> what's it called when you miss him  
> but you don't want him back  
> but you still wanna end up with him  
> but if he asked you'd say no  
> but you can't imagine dating anyone else

Stats:

- Around 2.8k views.
- 68 likes.
- 11 shares.
- Strong emotional resonance.
- Strong "this is literally me" reaction.

Why it worked:

- Contradiction stack.
- Low cognitive load.
- Emotionally irrational logic.
- No explanation.
- Highly recognizable internal conflict.
- Feels like a private thought, not advice.

### Important Positive Signal Reel

Text:

> normalize saying "this is not how I want to be loved" and move on...

Stats:

- Around 1,959 views.
- 83 likes.
- 31 saves.
- 11 shares.
- 96.9% non-followers.
- Average watch time: 7 seconds on a 12-second reel.
- Skip rate: 24.2%.
- Reels tab: 80% traffic.
- Instagram expanded distribution multiple times instead of killing it early.

Interpretation:

This was not a viral breakthrough, but it was the first strong positive algorithmic signal.

What mattered:

- The algorithm did not kill the reel immediately.
- Broader distribution happened.
- Saves and shares improved.
- Retention improved.
- Audience clustering improved slightly.

## Audience Clustering Observations

Countries frequently appearing:

- United States.
- India.
- Thailand.
- Australia.
- Germany.
- Canada.

Interpretation:

- The United States consistently appearing is a positive signal.
- India is overrepresented because Instagram often tests new English-speaking emotional content there.
- The cluster is not fully wrong, but it is still unstable.
- Overly generic motivational content may attract a broad low-quality audience.
- Psychologically nuanced, emotionally intelligent content may attract a stronger Western audience over time.

## Current Goal

Find repeatable content patterns that:

- Improve retention.
- Increase saves and shares.
- Strengthen audience clustering.
- Build an emotionally resonant female audience.
- Preserve Andrum's unique identity.

## Content Variables To Track

When analyzing Reels, track:

- Hook type.
- Emotional mechanism.
- Specificity.
- Text density.
- Visual emotional energy.
- Cognitive load.
- Shareability.
- Emotional positioning.
- Pacing.

## Working Content Patterns

More likely to work:

- Short hooks.
- Instantly understandable emotion.
- Hyper-specific behaviors.
- Shame and self-awareness.
- Contradiction.
- Emotionally exposing thoughts.
- "This is literally me" feeling.
- Phone checking, rereading, waiting, drafting, deleting.
- Panic before certainty.
- Wanting reassurance while distrusting reassurance.

Less likely to work:

- Long text blocks.
- Overexplaining.
- Abstract nervous system education.
- Clinical language.
- Generic self-help coaching tone.
- Polished inspirational language.
- Content that requires the audience to think before they feel.

## Practical Content Rule

Start with the emotionally recognizable behavior.

Then, if needed, lightly imply the nervous system underneath.

Do not lead with education.

Weak:

> Anxious attachment can create hypervigilance around inconsistent communication.

Strong:

> you read "haha yeah" and suddenly you're investigating the emotional temperature of the entire relationship

## Current Creative Direction

Short Reels with:

- Simple visuals.
- Emotionally charged text on screen.
- Minimal editing.
- Internal monologue format.
- Strong emotional recognition.
- Low cognitive load.

Visuals should support the feeling, not compete with the text.

## Format Differentiation

### Reels

Reels need:

- Immediate emotional hit.
- Short hook-first writing.
- Low cognitive load.
- Emotional recognition first.

### Carousels

Carousels can be slightly deeper.

They should include:

- Clear structure.
- Simple nervous system or body insight.
- Swipe momentum.
- One idea per slide.

## Decision Rule

If a Reel idea sounds educational, translate it into:

- A behavior.
- A private thought.
- A contradiction.
- A body reaction.
- A moment someone would be embarrassed to admit.

The final content should feel like:

> "holy shit this is me"

Not:

> "interesting, I learned something."
