# What Makes This Relatable

Use this when judging whether an Andrum post feels emotionally true.

## Relatability Comes From Specificity

Generic:

> Anxious attachment can make you fear abandonment.

Relatable:

> They take longer than usual to reply, and suddenly you are rereading the last three messages like there is a hidden warning inside them.

The second one works because it shows the actual moment.

## The Four Layers Of A Relatable Andrum Post

### 1. The outside event

This should be small and ordinary.

Examples:

- They did not reply.
- Their tone changed.
- They said they were busy.
- They watched your story.
- They were warm in person and distant later.

### 2. The body reaction

This makes the post feel real.

Examples:

- Your stomach drops.
- Your chest gets tight.
- Your breath gets shallow.
- Your body starts buzzing.
- You cannot sit still.

### 3. The private meaning

This is where people feel seen.

Examples:

- "They are leaving."
- "I did something wrong."
- "I am too much."
- "I need to fix this."
- "I knew this would happen."

### 4. The gentle reframe

This gives relief without becoming a lecture.

Examples:

- Your body may be reacting to the fear of losing connection, not just the text.
- You can want reassurance without making yourself wrong for needing it.
- Mixed signals are confusing because they give warmth without safety.

## Why The Reddit Language Works

### It uses tiny details

The strongest lines mention timing, tone, word choice, short texts, silence, and specific behaviors.

Why it works:

People recognize their own exact habits.

### It admits the embarrassing part

Relatable content often says the thought people hide.

Examples:

- "I write messages I never send."
- "I like being liked."
- "I know it is small, but it feels huge."
- "I want to ask, but I do not want to seem needy."

Why it works:

Shame drops when the hidden thing is named without judgment.

### It contains contradiction

Contradiction makes content emotionally sticky.

Examples:

- They care, but they do not choose.
- It feels intimate, but there is no clarity.
- You know it hurts, but you still want them.
- You accepted it mentally, but your body is still waiting.

Why it works:

Real relationship pain is rarely clean.

### It makes the body part of the story

The body language is crucial for Andrum.

Why it works:

People do not experience anxiety as an idea. They experience it as chest, stomach, hands, breath, heat, urgency, and freeze.

### It does not over-explain

The best lines stop once the recognition lands.

Weak:

> This is because your attachment system has been activated by perceived relational threat.

Strong:

> Your body heard silence and started preparing to be left.

## Relatability Checklist

Before publishing, ask:

- Is there a real moment, not just a concept?
- Is there a body sensation?
- Is there a private thought people are usually embarrassed to say?
- Is the language simple enough to send to a friend?
- Is there a contradiction or emotional turn?
- Does it avoid diagnosing the reader?
- Does it feel like recognition before advice?

## Andrum Golden Rule

Do not explain the pattern from above.

Stand inside the moment with her.

Then offer one breath of space.
