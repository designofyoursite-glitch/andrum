# Carousel Strategy

Carousels should teach one idea in a saveable way.

## Carousel Formula

1. Cover: strong hook or useful promise.
2. Problem: name what the audience is struggling with.
3. Shift: reframe how to think about it.
4. Steps: give 3 to 5 clear points.
5. Example: make it concrete.
6. Final slide: recap or action step.

## Best Carousel Types

- Step-by-step frameworks.
- Common mistakes.
- Checklists.
- Before and after examples.
- Swipe files.
- "How to think about..." posts.

## Slide Rules

- One idea per slide.
- Short lines.
- Clear hierarchy.
- Make slides easy to skim.
- Do not cram the whole essay into the carousel.

## Quality Check

- Would someone save this?
- Does the cover create curiosity?
- Does each slide move the idea forward?
- Is the final action simple?
