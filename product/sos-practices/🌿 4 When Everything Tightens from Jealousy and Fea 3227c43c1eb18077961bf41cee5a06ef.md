🌿

*For: jealousy spike / fear of abandonment / anxious attachment activation*

</aside>

---

## Context

**State Description:**

The body is tense. Breathing is short. There's a knot inside.

Fear arises — of being replaced, forgotten, or unseen.

Anxious attachment becomes painfully activated.

**Key Goal:**

To calm the body, reduce the intensity of jealousy, and restore a sense of inner safety and self-worth.

**When to Use:**

- When thoughts like "he'll pull away," "I'm not important," or "I'll be replaced" appear
- During jealousy spikes or mild to moderate triggers
- Best used in a quiet space where you can feel your body

---

## Breathing Technique: Extended Exhale Breathing (4–6)

**How:**

- Inhale through the nose for 4
- Exhale slowly for 6 (through the mouth or nose, whichever feels easier)
- Slow and steady throughout

**Why it works:**

- Jealousy is high sympathetic activation with an abandonment signal underneath — the longer exhale tells the body: *the threat has passed. I am safe right now.*
- It reduces that internal "I'm about to lose something" urgency without forcing calm
- Gives the nervous system a steady, repeatable reassurance cue

**Optional deepen:** If the activation feels very intense, extend the exhale to **4–8** for a stronger parasympathetic response.

**Layered option:**

- On inhale: *"I am safe."*
- On exhale: *"Even if I feel scared."*

---

## Core Affirmations (5 strongest)

1. **"I am not losing anything that is truly mine to keep."**
2. **"I belong to myself, first."**
3. **"I can feel this fear without becoming it."**
4. **"My worth doesn't live inside someone else's attention."**
5. **"I can come back to myself, even from here."**

---

## Additional Affirmation Options

1. **"Jealousy is a signal, not a verdict."**
2. **"I don't have to compete to be worthy of love."**
3. **"The steadiness I'm looking for is already inside me."**
4. **"What I bring cannot be replicated."**

## 5-Minute Guided Meditation Script

*"Beneath the fear of losing someone, there is almost always a longing to be deeply seen."*

### Introduction (30 seconds)

Something has been stirred in you — it feels like jealousy.

It may feel like a mix of anger, or a sadness, or that quiet sting of comparison — the feeling that someone else has more, is more.

Whatever shape it's taking— you're in the right place.

There's nothing we need to figure out right now.

You're here. And we're going to let the breath bring you back.

---

### Grounding Arrival (45 seconds)

*You can close your eyes now, or simply let your gaze fall soft and low.* 

Pause

Bring your attention to your body.

*Pause*

Feel the weight of you — wherever you're sitting or lying, let that surface hold you.

*Pause*

Take a slow breath in, and as you exhale, see if anything can soften. Your hands. Your belly. The space around your heart.

*Pause*

Place a hand somewhere on your body — your chest, your stomach — wherever feels like it needs a little more presence.

*Pause*

---

### Extended Exhale Breathing (2–3 minutes)

Now let's come to the breath.

We're going to use a simple, powerful rhythm: **inhale for 4, exhale for 6.**

Every slow exhale is a whisper to your body: you're still here. You're still okay.

Let's begin.

*Inhale… 2, 3, 4*

*Exhale… 2, 3, 4, 5, 6*

*Inhale… 2, 3, 4*

*Exhale… 2, 3, 4, 5, 6*

*Inhale… 2, 3, 4*

*Exhale… 2, 3, 4, 5, 6*

*Inhale… 2, 3, 4*

*Exhale… 2, 3, 4, 5, 6*

**Let the exhale carry what you've been holding.**

*(Quieter)*

Inhale (sound of breath for 4)

Exhale (sound of breath for 6)

If this feeling of jealousy rises again — that's okay. *Let the exhale take just a little bit more of it away. Even 1% is enough*

You're not trying to stop feeling. You're just giving your body a different signal.

*Inhale… 2, 3, 4*

*Exhale… 2, 3, 4, 5, 6*

*Inhale… 2, 3, 4*

*Exhale… 2, 3, 4, 5, 6*

*Pause 30 seconds — continue rhythm (sound of breath in background)*

---

### Affirmations (1 minute) *(sound of breath softly in background)*

As the breath settles in, here are some phrases. You can repeat them silently or just let them land.

*Pause between each*

**"***What I bring cannot be replicated.***"**

*Pause*

**"My worth doesn't live inside someone else's attention."**

*Pause*

**"***I can be my own safe place***."**

*Pause*

With each exhale, let the words land a little deeper.

**"What I bring cannot be replicated."**

*Pause*

**"My worth doesn't live inside someone else's attention."**

*Pause*

**"I can be my own safe place."**

*Pause*

*Inhale… (silent count of 4)*

*Exhale… (silent count of 6)*

Pause 20 seconds

---

### Self-Energy Check-In (30 seconds)

Let the breath return to its natural rhythm now.

*Pause*

Where is the jealousy sitting in your body now? Has it moved at all — even a little?

*Pause*

Can you feel any curiosity about this feeling — rather than just being gripped by it?

*Pause*

If yes, let that be enough. If not — that's okay too. You showed up for yourself. And that matters more than you know.

---

### Closing (30 seconds)

Jealousy doesn't mean you're too much, or that something is wrong with you.

It means you *care*. Deeply.

And underneath— there is love. There is longing. There is *you*, wanting to be seen

That part of you deserves your care too.

Pause

Let's take one last slow breath in together…

*Pause*

And let it go.

*Pause*

Whenever you're ready, gently open your eyes.

Welcome back.

---
