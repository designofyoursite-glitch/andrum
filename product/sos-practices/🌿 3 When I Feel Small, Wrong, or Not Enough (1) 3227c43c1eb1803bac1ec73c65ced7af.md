# 🌿 3. When I Feel Small, Wrong, or "Not Enough" (1)

<aside>
🌿

**Guided Meditation Script | 5–10 Minutes**

*For: shame / self-criticism / "not enough" / feeling small*

</aside>

---

## Context

**State Description:**

Shame and guilt rise, along with a deep sense of inadequacy. An inner voice whispers — quietly or loudly: "I'm not like that. I ruin everything. I don't belong." The body contracts. There's a desire to hide or disappear.

**Key Goal:**

To gently restore a sense of inner worth, soften what has tightened, calm the inner critic, and reconnect with dignity.

**When to Use:**

- When you feel shame or self-criticism after communication, a mistake, or an emotional reaction
- Helpful as a regular practice to strengthen self-connection
- Suitable for the start of the day or after difficult situations

---

## Breathing Technique: Heart-Centered Coherent Breathing (4–4)

**How:**

- Hands on heart (optional)
- Inhale 4
- Exhale 4
- Imagine breath moving in and out of the chest / heart space

**Why it works:**

- Shame contracts the chest and drops posture — this breath gently reopens the heart area
- The equal inhale and exhale creates expansion and warmth without further sedating a collapse state
- Restores a sense of dignity rather than just calming anxiety

---

## Core Affirmations (5 strongest)

1. **"I am allowed to exist exactly as I am."**
2. **"I can notice this voice without believing everything it says."**
3. **"I can offer myself the same kindness I'd give someone I love."**
4. **"Getting it wrong doesn't make me wrong."**
5. **"I can come back to myself, every time."**

---

## Additional Affirmation Options

1. **"The part of me that criticises is trying to protect me. I don't have to follow it."**
2. **"My worth isn't earned. It's already here."**
3. **"I have always been doing the best I could with what I had."**
4. **"I belong here, even when it doesn't feel that way."**

## Guided Meditation Script

*"You don't have to earn your way back to yourself. You're already there."*

### Introduction (30 seconds)

There's a story running today. A voice thats saying, "I got it wrong. I am wrong. I'm not enough."

We don't have to fix that story right now.

Let's just set it down for a moment— together.

No need to change or explain it — we're just going to find our way back, gently.

---

### Grounding Arrival (45 seconds)

Let's start by feeling where your body is right now.

Notice the presence of you. The simple, physical fact that you are *here* — occupying space, taking up room, *allowed* to be here.

*Pause*

Feel where your body meets the ground beneath you.

*Can you feel that connection — solid and still?*

*Pause*

Let your hands rest somewhere soft — maybe on your lap, or place them gently on your chest.

*Pause*

And just notice, although your inner critic is loud right now, your body is here, holding you in this moment.

*Pause*

---

### Heart-Centered Coherent Breathing (2–3 minutes)

Now, let's bring some breath into the chest.

We're going to breathe together in a slow, even rhythm — inhale for 4, exhale for 4. Equal in and out. There's no rush. No right way to do this.

If you have your hands on your chest, let them rest there. Imagine the breath moving in and out of your heart space — not just your lungs, but that whole area softening.

Let's breathe together.

*Inhale... 2... 3... 4*

*Exhale... 2... 3... 4*

*Inhale... 2... 3... 4*

*Exhale... 2... 3... 4*

*Inhale... 2... 3... 4*

*Exhale... 2... 3... 4*

*(Sound of breathing x4 approx)*

If your inner voice is still a little loud — that's okay. We're not trying to silence it. We're just giving the body a different experience right now.

Keep following the rhythm. Let your chest slowly, gently open.

*Inhale... 2... 3... 4*

*Exhale... 2... 3... 4*

*(Sound of breath in background for 20 seconds)*

*Pause*

---

### Affirmations (1 minute)

As we keep breathing, I'm going to offer you some phrases.

Say them silently, or let them just land — whatever feels right.

*Pause between each*

**"I am allowed to exist exactly as I am."**

*Pause*

**"I can notice this voice without believing everything it says."**

*Pause*

**"I can be gentle with myself."**

*Pause*

**"I am allowed to exist exactly as I am."**

*Pause*

**"I can notice this voice without believing everything it says."**

*Pause*

**"I can be gentle with myself."**

*Pause*

*20 seconds breath*

---

### Self-Energy Check-In (30 seconds)

Let the breath return to its natural rhythm now, and as it does — lets look inward, gently.

*Pause*

Can you notice a softening somewhere? Can you find even a whisper of compassion for yourself right now?

*Pause*

If yes, let that be enough. If not, that's okay.

This was an act of kindness toward yourself and this is more than enough. 

---

### Closing (30 seconds)

Your inner voice had a lot to say today.

And yet — you chose to come here anyway. A quiet, steady part of you that the voice couldn't drown out.

*That's* the part that knows your worth — your courage, and your capacity to keep choosing yourself.

And it's always here. Whenever you need to find your way back.

Let's take one last slow breath in together…

*Pause*

And let it go.

*Pause*

Whenever you're ready, gently open your eyes.

Welcome back, beautiful.
