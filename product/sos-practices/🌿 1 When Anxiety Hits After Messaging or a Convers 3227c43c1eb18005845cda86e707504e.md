# 🌿 1. When Anxiety Hits After Messaging or a Conversation (1)

## Context

**State Description:**

A message, a pause in response, a cold tone, or something left unsaid hit harder than you expected. Your body tightens, your breath becomes shallow, panic rises, and it feels like something went wrong. Thoughts start racing. Anxiety comes in waves.

**Key Goal:**

To bring support back into the body, reduce nervous system reactivity, gently come out of the trigger, and feel grounded again.

**When to Use:**

- Right after receiving a triggering message or conversation
- Ideally within 10 minutes of activation
- Can also be used daily as a stabilizing ritual

---

## Breathing Technique: Physiological Sigh → 4–6 Breathing

### Physiological sigh (a couple of rounds)

**How:**

- Inhale through the nose
- Take a small second inhale to “top up”
- Long, slow exhale through the mouth

### 4–6 breathing (inhale 4, exhale 6)

**How:**

- Inhale through the nose for 4
- Exhale slowly for 6 (through the mouth or nose, whichever feels easiest)

**Why it works:**

- The physiological sigh helps discharge the sharp edge of activation quickly
- The longer exhale in 4–6 breathing gives your nervous system a steady signal of safety
- Counting gently anchors attention without feeling overly controlled

## Guided Meditation Script

*“Between stimulus and response there is a space. In that space is our power to choose our response.”*

### Introduction (30 seconds)

**Something has just triggered you: a message, or a comment in a conversation you’ve had.** Your thoughts have started racing and you're feeling anxious. That's okay. There’s nothing we need to solve right now.

You're here, and we’re going to help your body settle.

---

### Body Scan (1 minute)

Let's start by noticing what's happening in your body in this moment.

*Pause*

Maybe there’s some tightness in your chest.

Maybe butterflies in your belly.

Or tingling in your hands.

Maybe there’s tension in your neck or shoulders.

Take a moment to sense how your body feels.

Welcome everything.

There’s no need to change it.

"Yes, this is here. And we can be with it"

*Pause 30 seconds*

---

### Physiological Sigh (30–45 seconds)

Now let’s come to the breath.

We’ll start with **a few rounds of a physiological sigh**.

Full inhale through the nose…

Then at the top take one more sip of air…

And then a long, slow exhale out through the mouth.

Again.

(Sound of breath in background separately)

Inhale…in again….

And long exhale.

Inhale…in again…

Exhale

Inhale…in again…

And let the exhale pour out. - 

---

### 4–6 Breathing (4 minutes)

Now we’ll move into a steadier rhythm.

**Inhale for 4…**

And **exhale for 6…**

Stay with my voice for a few rounds, and let the counting hold you.

*Inhale… 2, 3, 4*

*Exhale… 2, 3, 4, 5, 6*

*Inhale… 2, 3, 4*

*Exhale… 2, 3, 4, 5, 6*

*Inhale… 2, 3, 4*

*Exhale… 2, 3, 4, 5, 6*

*Inhale… 2, 3, 4*

*Exhale… 2, 3, 4, 5, 6*

**Continue with the rhythm, and allow your body soften into it.**

*(Quieter count for 30 seconds)* 

Inhale (sound of breath for 4)

Exhale (sound of breath for 6)

If you notice your mind wandering, gently bring your attention back to the count.

*Inhale… 2, 3, 4*

*Exhale… 2, 3, 4, 5, 6*

Pause 30 seconds

### Self-Energy Check-In (30 seconds)

After the next exhale, start to return to your natural breath. Lets take a moment now: can you notice a change towards this anxious feeling?

*Pause*

Can you feel curious about it? Compassionate?

*Pause*

If yes, let that feeling expand just a little. If not, that's okay too. You've honoured this feeling. And that's enough.

---

### Closing (30 seconds)

Your body knows how to return to calm.

You’re learning to help it get there.

Before we go, pause and give yourself a moment of appreciation for being here.

Let’s take one more deep breath in together…

And let it go.

*Pause*

And gently open your eyes.

Welcome back.
