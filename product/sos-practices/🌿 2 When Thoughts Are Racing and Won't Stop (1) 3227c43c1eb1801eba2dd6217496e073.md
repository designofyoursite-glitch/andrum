# 🌿 2. When Thoughts Are Racing and Won't Stop (1)

<aside>
🌿

**Guided Meditation Script | 5–10 Minutes**

*For: obsessive thinking / mental overdrive / racing thoughts*

</aside>

---

*“When you can’t think your way to safety, you can breathe your way there.”*

### Introduction (30 seconds)

Your mind is running at full speed right now.

Maybe you're replaying a conversation, or imagining how something might go. Maybe you've been trying to stop the thoughts — and somehow that's only made them louder.

That's okay. You don't need to fix it. You don't need to figure anything out in the next few minutes.

You just need to come back to your body. And I'll help you do that.

---

### Grounding Arrival (45 seconds)

Let's start by arriving exactly where we are.

Feel wherever your body is making contact with the ground, the seat, your bed. Just notice the weight of you — the simple, solid fact that your body is *here*, even while your mind is somewhere else.

*Pause*

Can you let your jaw soften? You might not have realised how tightly you were holding it.

*Pause*

Can you let your shoulders drop? Could you relax your belly, just a little?

*Pause*

You don't have to calm your mind right now. Just feel your body. Nothing more than that.

Pause

---

### Box Breathing  (2-3 minutes)

Now let's notice the breath.

Inhale and exhale.

We're going to breathe together in a box pattern: inhale for 4, hold for 4, exhale for 4, hold for 4. Let the structure hold you and simply follow my voice.

*Inhale... 2... 3... 4*

*Hold... 2... 3... 4*

*Exhale... 2... 3... 4*

*Hold... 2... 3... 4*

*Inhale... 2... 3... 4*

*Hold... 2... 3... 4*

*Exhale... 2... 3... 4*

*Hold... 2... 3... 4*

*Inhale... 2... 3... 4*

*Hold... 2... 3... 4*

*Exhale... 2... 3... 4*

*Hold... 2... 3... 4*

If a thought comes in, that's okay. You don't have to push it away. Just bring yourself back to the count.

*Inhale... 2... 3... 4*

*Hold... 2... 3... 4*

*Exhale... 2... 3... 4*

*Hold... 2... 3... 4*

(Quieter)

Inhale (silent count)

Hold (silent count)

Exhale (silent count)

Hold (silent count)

Keep following the rhythm. Let your body soften into it.

*Pause 20 seconds — continue the rhythm (sound of breath in background)*

---

### Closing (1 minute)

Gently let the breath return to its natural rhythm now.

*Pause*

Lets bring our attention slowly downward. Crown of your head... your chest... your belly... your hands, your legs, your feet.

*Pause*

Just notice where you've landed. What does your body feel like right now, compared to when we started?

*Pause*

Maybe there's a little more space. Maybe just a small softening. That's enough.

You showed up for yourself. You interrupted the loop. And each time you do this, you're teaching your mind there's another way.

Take one more slow breath in together...

*Pause*

And let it go.

*Pause*

Whenever you're ready, gently open your eyes. Welcome back.

## Affirmation Bank

### Interrupting the loop

- *"I don't have to follow every thought that arrives."*
- *"My mind is busy. My body is still here."*
- *"Thinking about it more won't make it clearer right now."*
- *"This thought has passed through before. It will pass through again."*
- *"I notice I'm thinking. I don't have to keep going."*
- *"My thoughts are guests, not residents."*
- *"I can observe this without becoming it."*

### Returning to the present

- *"Right now, in this moment, nothing is actually wrong."*
- *"I am here. In my body. In this breath."*
- *"The present moment is quieter than my mind."*
- *"I can come back. I always can."*
- *"This breath is real. This moment is real."*
- *"My body is already here. I'm just catching up."*
- *"There is more stillness in me than noise."*

### Softening the grip

- *"I don't need to figure this out right now."*
- *"I can put this down, just for a few minutes."*
- *"I am more than the thoughts I'm having."*
- *"I can let this be unresolved and still be okay."*
- *"I can rest my mind the way I rest my body."*
- *"Clarity comes when I stop chasing it."*
- *"I don't have to resolve this to be okay right now."*

### Short bridges (repeatable during breath holds)

- *"I pause."*
- *"I return."*
- *"Just this breath."*
- *"I am here."*
- *"I notice."*
- *"I soften."*
- *"I land."*
