# 🌿 6. When I Freeze and Feel Nothing (1)

<aside>
🌿

**Guided Meditation Script | 5–10 Minutes**

*For: emotional numbness / freeze response / disconnection / shutdown*

</aside>

---

*"The body doesn't disappear in freeze. It just goes very, very quiet. And quiet can be reached."*

---

## Context

**State Description:**

Emotions seem to have switched off. The body feels heavy — or completely empty. It's hard to feel anger, joy, even anxiety. The world has gone muted. There's a flatness where feeling used to be.

**Key Goal:**

To gently thaw the body, restore sensation and warmth, and come back from the "I disappear" state — with patience and without pressure.

**When to Use:**

- When you feel numb, empty, or disconnected from yourself
- After a strong trigger has passed and left nothing behind
- Can be done sitting or lying down, in a calm and quiet moment
- Go slowly — freeze requires gentleness above all else

---

## Breathing Technique: Dirga — Three-Part Breath

**Belly → Ribcage → Chest (and back down)**

**How:**

- Inhale slowly, filling the belly first... then the ribcage... then the chest
- Exhale slowly from the chest... through the ribcage... back down through the belly
- Think of filling a glass from the bottom up, and emptying from the top down
- Keep it very slow and without force — this is about progressive reawakening, not depth

**Why it works:**

- Freeze is a shutdown response — the nervous system went quiet to protect you. Forcing it awake risks a re-freeze.
- The three-part breath gently reanimates the body layer by layer, starting with the most primal — the belly — and moving upward
- It avoids overstimulating a system that is already in protection mode

**Optional warming layer:** Imagine warmth spreading from your hands into your belly with each inhale — like thawing, very slowly, from the inside out.

## 5-Minute Guided Meditation Script

*"The body doesn't disappear in freeze. It just goes very, very quiet. And quiet can be reached."*

### Introduction (30 seconds)

You might be here because you can't feel anything right now.

Not sadness. Not anxiety. Not even anger.

Just... nothing. You feel like you should be hurt or upset, but the emotions won't come.

There’s just a heavy.. absence.

*All we're going to do is sit here together for a few minutes and let the body find its way back.*

---

### Arriving (2 minutes)

Let's start with something very simple.

Notice the weight of your body — wherever you're sitting or lying. Just the physical fact of you: here, taking up space.

*Pause*

Feel where your body meets the ground beneath you.

*Pause*

Now — let's explore the surface of our body with our hands.

If you'd like, bring your fingertips to the top of your head.

Just softly tap and notice the sensation

*Pause*

Slowly, let your hands move down to your face —Gently placing then on your forehead, your cheeks, your jaw. Feel the temperature, the pressure, even just the awareness of touch.

*Pause*

Now bring your hands down to your shoulders. You can squeeze them softly and maybe even give them a massage. 

*Pause*

When you're ready, bring your hands to your belly.

Gently with your fingertips find the edge of your ribs — Press in softly.

*just enough to feel something solid. Now you can move to the sides, maybe the centre. A little pressure*

*Pause*

Just noticing what's there.

*Pause*

Now let your hands move to your thighs. You can tap them gently or squeeze. 

*Pause*

We're just reminding the body that you're still in it.

---

### Dirga — Three-Part Breath (3 minutes)

Now let's bring the breath in

We're going to breathe in three parts.

First, into the belly. Just let it soften and expand.

Then, let the breath continue into the ribcage — feel the sides widen.

Then, all the way up into the chest.

And exhale slowly from the chest... through the ribcage... back down through the belly.

Let's try it together.

*Inhale into belly... ribcage... chest...*

*Exhale from chest... ribcage... belly...*

*Inhale... belly... ribcage... chest...*

*Exhale... chest... ribcage... belly...*

*(Sound of slow, gentle breath)*

*Inhale into belly...*

*Ribcage...*

*Chest...*

*Exhale...*

*Chest...*

*Ribcage...*

*Belly...*

If you'd like — as you inhale, imagine warmth rising with the breath. Belly... ribcage... chest. Spreading slowly upward, layer by layer.

*(Sound of breath for 30 seconds)*

*Pause*

---

### Coming Back (1 minute)

Let the breath return to its own rhythm now.

*Pause*

Notice if anything has shifted — even the smallest thing. Is the more sensation somewhere?

*Pause*

*Whatever you feel, it's enough.*

*Pause*

Sometimes the body goes quiet to keep us safe. There is nothing wrong. It's just looking after you.

And you came here to gently find your way back.

Let's take one last breath together.

Slowly in...

*Pause*

And slowly out.

*Pause*

Whenever you're ready, gently open your eyes.

Welcome back, love.

---

**Total time: 5–10 minutes**

<aside>
💡

**For deep freeze:** If numbness feels very heavy, try rubbing your hands together to generate warmth before you begin, or hold a warm drink against your palms. Sensation in the hands can be a gentle invitation back into the body.
