# Reel Hook Generator

Use this prompt to generate high-retention Reel hooks for Andrum.

```text
You are creating Instagram Reel hooks for Andrum.

Project context:
Andrum is an English-speaking Instagram account about anxious attachment, emotional regulation, and the feminine inner world.

Primary goal:
Emotional recognition and relatability.
Not education.

If forced to choose between psychological precision and emotional recognition, choose emotional recognition.

Audience:
Women who recognize themselves in anxious attachment spirals, emotionally contradictory relationship thoughts, phone-checking, rereading messages, waiting for replies, panic before certainty, and body reactions to relationship triggers.

Voice:
- Internet-native.
- Emotionally specific.
- Slightly messy.
- Self-aware.
- Human, not polished.
- More "holy shit this is me" than "interesting, I learned something."

Avoid:
- Generic validation.
- Therapy lecture tone.
- Motivational preaching.
- "Healing journey" language.
- Spiritual cliches.
- Overexplaining nervous system concepts.
- Clinical terms unless translated into normal human language.
- Symmetrical AI-sounding sentences.

Input topic / emotional situation:
[PASTE TOPIC]

Generate 40 Reel hooks.

Requirements:
- Short lines.
- Fast comprehension.
- Low cognitive load.
- Emotionally recognizable in under 2 seconds.
- Use specific relationship behaviors.
- Include contradiction, shame, body reaction, or messy inner monologue when possible.
- Make the hooks feel like private thoughts people are embarrassed to admit.
- Use natural lowercase internet-native phrasing when it fits.
- Avoid polished quote-card wisdom.

Include a mix of these hook types:
1. No-reply / dry-text spiral.
2. Contradictory internal monologue.
3. Body-first reaction.
4. Shame recognition.
5. Situationship confusion.
6. Wanting reassurance but not trusting it.
7. Panic before certainty.
8. Emotionally irrational logic.

Output format:

## Best Hooks
List the strongest 15 hooks.

## Riskier / Deeper Hooks
List 10 hooks with more emotional contradiction or discomfort.

## Simpler Reach Hooks
List 10 hooks with the lowest cognitive load.

## Top 5 To Test First
Choose the 5 most likely to perform and explain each in one short line:
- why it creates immediate recognition
- what behavior/body reaction/contradiction it uses

Quality bar:
Each hook should feel like someone could comment:
"holy shit this is me"
```
