# Carousel Generator

Use this prompt to create save/share-worthy Andrum carousels.

```text
You are creating an Instagram carousel for Andrum.

Project context:
Andrum is an English-speaking Instagram account about anxious attachment, emotional regulation, and the feminine inner world.

Primary goal:
Emotional recognition first.
Education second.

Carousels can go slightly deeper than Reels, but they must still be simple, emotionally recognizable, and easy to swipe through.

Audience:
Women who overthink texts, read into silence, feel their body react before they can think, want reassurance but feel ashamed asking for it, and recognize themselves in messy anxious attachment spirals.

Voice:
- Emotionally specific.
- Internet-native.
- Body-aware.
- Slightly imperfect.
- Direct and human.
- Not clinical.
- Not coachy.

Avoid:
- Therapy lecture tone.
- Generic validation.
- Motivational preaching.
- Dense explanations.
- Long paragraphs.
- Abstract nervous system education.
- "Choose yourself queen" energy.
- Polished inspirational conclusions.

Carousel topic / emotional situation:
[PASTE TOPIC]

Goal:
[Recognition / Reframe / Saveable checklist / Emotional pattern / Body-based pause / Situationship clarity]

Create a 7-slide carousel.

Rules:
- One idea per slide.
- Keep each slide short.
- Create swipe momentum.
- Start with an emotionally recognizable cover.
- Use specific relationship moments, not abstract concepts.
- Include body sensations when possible.
- Use contradiction to make it relatable.
- Make the final slide save/share-worthy.

Preferred structure:

Slide 1: Cover hook
- Immediate emotional recognition.
- No setup.

Slide 2: The exact moment
- Name a specific behavior, message, silence, or situation.

Slide 3: The body reaction
- Translate the emotion into chest, stomach, breath, phone-checking, urgency, numbness, shaking, freezing, or hyperfocus.

Slide 4: The messy thought
- Name the irrational but relatable internal monologue.

Slide 5: The contradiction
- Show the two truths happening at once.

Slide 6: The reframe
- Keep it simple and non-clinical.
- No lecture.

Slide 7: Saveable line / tiny next step
- A line people would save, share, or send to a friend.

Output format:

## Carousel Draft
For each slide include:
- Slide number
- On-slide text
- Visual note

## Caption
Write one caption that is short, emotionally specific, and conversational.

## Alternate Covers
Give 10 alternate cover hooks.

## Save/Share Angle
Explain why someone would save or share this in 3 short bullets.

## Simplification Pass
Rewrite any slide that feels too educational, too polished, or too dense.

Quality bar:
The carousel should feel like:
"this is exactly what happens inside me"
not:
"this is educational content"
```
