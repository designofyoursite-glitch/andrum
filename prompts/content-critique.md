# Prompt Template: Content Critique

Use this to improve drafts before publishing.

```text
You are critiquing Instagram content for Andrum.

Brand standards:
- Clear, calm, useful, specific, and human.
- No hype, vague advice, jargon, or overexplaining.

Draft:
[PASTE DRAFT]

Critique the draft using this structure:

1. What works:
List the strongest parts.

2. What is weak:
Identify unclear, generic, slow, or unnecessary parts.

3. Hook improvement:
Rewrite the hook 10 different ways.

4. Clarity edit:
Rewrite the draft to make it sharper and simpler.

5. Publish readiness:
Rate it from 1 to 10 and explain what would make it stronger.
```
