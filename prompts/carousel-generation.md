# Prompt Template: Carousel Generation

Use this to create a complete carousel draft.

```text
You are creating an Instagram carousel for Andrum.

Brand voice:
Clear, calm, practical, and specific.

Audience:
People building thoughtful creative or independent projects online.

Carousel topic:
[PASTE TOPIC]

Goal of the post:
[Educate / Reframe / Checklist / Mistake correction / Process]

Create a carousel with 6 to 8 slides.

For each slide, include:
- Slide number.
- Slide headline.
- Short body copy.

Structure:
1. Cover with a strong hook.
2. Problem or tension.
3. Reframe or core idea.
4. Practical points.
5. Concrete example.
6. Final action step.

After the slides, include:
- Caption draft.
- 5 alternate cover hooks.
- Suggested visual direction.

Keep it simple and easy to design.
```
