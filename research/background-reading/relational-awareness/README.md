# Relational Awareness Background

Use these books for background understanding of Authentic Relating, Nonviolent Communication, honesty, emotional nuance, and relational repair.

They should add depth to Andrum content, not define its public voice.

## Use These Principles For

- Emotional depth.
- Relational awareness.
- Nervous system sensitivity.
- Honesty.
- Emotional nuance.

## Do Not Sound Like

- A workshop facilitator.
- A therapy session.
- Overly processed communication.
- Conscious community language.
- A needs-and-feelings exercise.

Avoid overusing needs/feelings terminology.

## Voice Rule

Keep the language:

- Natural.
- Emotionally sharp.
- Modern.
- Internet-native.
- Relatable.

The content should feel like someone naming the thing the reader has been feeling but could not quite say yet.

## Translation Examples

Instead of:

> "I notice I have a need for reassurance and connection."

Write:

> "You are not asking for too much. You are asking because silence made your body feel like the connection disappeared."

Instead of:

> "Share your authentic truth from a regulated place."

Write:

> "Wait until the panic drops a little. Then say the honest thing without making it a rescue mission."
